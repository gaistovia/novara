Support

Frequently asked questions

Are NOVARA devices 100% original?

Yes. Every device is certified genuine and sourced through authorised regional distribution, backed by a 12-month official warranty and IMEI verification you can check yourself.

What payment methods do you accept?

M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, HaloPesa, Mixx by Yas, Visa, Mastercard, bank transfer, and 0% interest installment plans for qualifying customers.

How fast is delivery?

Same-day within Dar es Salaam for orders before 2pm, and 24–72 hours nationwide. You'll receive a tracking link by SMS and WhatsApp.

Can I trade in my old phone?

Yes — bring your device in-store or send photos via WhatsApp for an instant valuation, deductible from your new purchase.

What is your return policy?

7-day easy returns on unopened or faulty devices, no questions asked. Warranty claims are handled directly through our service desk.
